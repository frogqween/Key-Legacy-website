FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What are your application requirements?

We require verifiable income of at least 3x monthly rent, satisfactory credit history, and no evictions in the past 4 years. Specific criteria may vary by property.

How long does the application process take?

Most applications are reviewed within 24-48 hours. We'll keep you informed throughout the process and notify you promptly of any decisions.

Do you charge application fees?

Application fees vary by property and cover background and credit checks. All fees are disclosed upfront before you submit your application.

Are pets allowed?

Many of our properties are pet-friendly, though restrictions may apply. Each property listing specifies pet policies, including any deposits or fees.
