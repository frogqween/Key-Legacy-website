about key legacy realty

building communities and creating homes throughout philadelphia

our mission

key legacy realty is a platform created to inform and update its audience about everything philly (suburbs included). from best places to dine; where to plan your next event; to pro tips from local businesses; we hope to create a virtual environment for all those that love this amazing community and would like to experience what it has to offer.

we are dedicated to connecting renters with quality homes and helping investors build their legacy in the philadelphia region.

the earnest key team

the earnest key team is committed to excellence in every interaction. we understand the philadelphia rental market because we live here. our team members are your neighbors, and we take pride in helping build strong, vibrant communities throughout the city.

whether you are buying, selling, investing, or renting, we are here to guide you through every step of the process with integrity and local expertise.
