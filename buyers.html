frequently asked questions

how do i know if i'm ready to buy?

you will know you are ready to buy a home after you have spoken with a mortgage professional (lender). a lender will be able to help you determine a monthly payment you are comfortable with as well as verify what you will need for a down payment and closing costs.

what are the first steps to buying a home?

the first steps to buying a home are getting pre-approved by a mortgage professional and connecting with a realtor.

what is the role of a realtor?

a realtor is the professional who guides you through the process of finding and closing on a home. a realtor also maintains a relationship with you throughout your homeownership. the realtor connects you with other vetted professionals you will need such as home inspectors and lenders.

besides the down payment, are there other financial considerations?

yes. in addition to the down payment, you will need to pay "closing costs". these include transfer taxes, property taxes, loan fees, and title fees. you will also pay for inspections and appraisals prior to settlement.

what happens after my offer is accepted?

once your offer is accepted, you will deposit your earnest money. you will also attend home inspections, order the appraisal, and submit your documents to the mortgage company. your agent will guide you through this process to the closing table.
